# EES 680 AI partner project: paper sign-ups

This repo holds the sign-ups for the EES 680 (Earth and Environmental Data Analysis) AI partner project, Fall 2026.

To pick a paper, browse the list at [nickmckay.org/eeda-papers](https://nickmckay.org/eeda-papers/) and click **Claim this paper**. That opens an issue here, and the paper shows as taken on the list.

- One paper per student. If two people claim the same paper, the earlier issue keeps it.
- To change your mind, close your issue and claim another.
- Issues here are public: your GitHub username and your choice are visible. If you'd rather not, ask Nick on Slack instead.
